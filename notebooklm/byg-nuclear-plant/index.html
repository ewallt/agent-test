About This Illustration

F.T. Wright uses the image of a nuclear power plant to reframe how we read biblical "destruction" texts. When the townspeople expel the technician whose constant vigilance kept the reactor safe, the explosion that follows is not his retaliation — it is simply what happens in his absence. Wright sees God in that technician: expelled, not retaliating; absent, not attacking.
