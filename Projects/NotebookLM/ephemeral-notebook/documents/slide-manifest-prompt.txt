---

WHAT THE MANIFEST IS
The manifest is a markdown file. Gemini reads it as a source and builds slides
from the content. Each slide has a title, two paragraphs of text, and an image
context description. The image context tells Gemini what to generate for the
visual — it always ends with the global visual style repeated verbatim.

---

*   **Image Context:** [Specific visual scene for this slide — 1–2 sentences

---

[Repeat for each slide]

---

RULES FOR GOOD SLIDES
- Each slide covers one idea fully — don't split a single point across slides
- Paragraph A sets up; Paragraph B pays off, deepens, or contrasts
- Image context should describe a specific scene or object, not an abstraction
  ("A chalkboard with the equation written on it" not "a mathematical concept")
- Titles should sound like chapter headings, not topic labels
- The global style phrase must appear at the end of every image context, word
  for word — Gemini uses it to maintain visual consistency across the deck
- Slide 1 should hook the reader; the final slide should land a clear takeaway

SLIDE COUNT
Aim for 8–12 slides unless I specify otherwise. More than 12 gets unwieldy;
fewer than 8 feels thin. A good default is 10.
