guidance: |
  Three battles, one video each. Apply the same lens to all three:
  why the battle was important, what was unique about it, surprising facts
  people might not know, and how it shifted the arc of the war.

  Battle of France (1940): The stunning speed of the German victory; the Maginot
  Line failure; Dunkirk as a near-catastrophe turned myth. Why did France fall so fast?

  D-Day (1944): The largest amphibious invasion in history. Deception operations
  (Operation Fortitude), near-failure at Omaha Beach, what could have gone differently.

  Battle of the Bulge (1944-45): Hitler's last major offensive in the West. The
  surprise, the siege of Bastogne, Patton's relief march. Why it nearly worked and
  why it ultimately sealed Germany's fate.
