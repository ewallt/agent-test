guidance: |
  Three battles, one video each. Apply the same lens to all three:
  why the battle was important, what was unique about it, surprising facts
  people might not know, and how it shifted the arc of the war.

  North Africa (1940-43): The Desert War — Rommel's brilliance, Montgomery at
  El Alamein, and why control of North Africa mattered strategically. The turning
  point that opened the door to Southern Europe.

  Battle of Sicily (1943): Operation Husky — the largest amphibious assault before
  D-Day. The Allied rivalry between Patton and Montgomery, the role of deception
  (Operation Mincemeat), and how Sicily led directly to Mussolini's fall.

  Battle of Greece (1940-41): Greece's unexpected resistance to the Italian invasion,
  then the swift German conquest. Churchill's costly decision to defend Greece,
  and the debate over whether it delayed Operation Barbarossa.
