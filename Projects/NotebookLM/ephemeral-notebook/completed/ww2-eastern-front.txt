guidance: |
  Three battles, one video each. Apply the same lens to all three:
  why the battle was important, what was unique about it, surprising facts
  people might not know, and how it shifted the arc of the war.

  Battle of Stalingrad (1942-43): The turning point of the Eastern Front. Urban
  warfare at its most brutal, the Soviet encirclement of the German 6th Army,
  Paulus's surrender. Why Stalingrad is considered the single most decisive battle
  of WW2 by many historians.

  Battle of Kursk (1943): The largest tank battle in history. Germany's last major
  offensive on the Eastern Front, the Soviet intelligence advantage, and why the
  Wehrmacht never recovered. The battle that gave the Soviets permanent initiative.

  Operation Bagration (1944): The massive Soviet summer offensive that destroyed
  Army Group Centre — largely unknown in the West despite being larger in scope
  than D-Day. Why it succeeded so completely, and its decisive role in ending the war.
