guidance: |
  Four battles, one video each. Apply the same lens to all four:
  why the battle was important, what was unique about it, surprising facts
  people might not know, and how it shifted the arc of the war.

  Pearl Harbor (1941): The attack that brought the US into the war. Japanese
  strategic miscalculation, the missing aircraft carriers, and the debate over
  what Roosevelt knew. Why the attack ultimately doomed Japan despite its tactical success.

  Battle of Midway (1942): The decisive turning point of the Pacific war. American
  code-breaking, the role of chance, the destruction of Japan's carrier fleet in
  five minutes. How a numerically inferior force won the most consequential naval
  battle in history.

  The Philippines — Fall and Recapture (1941-42 / 1944-45): Cover both halves as
  one story. The fall: Bataan, the Death March, MacArthur's escape and promise.
  The return: Leyte Gulf (the largest naval battle ever fought), the liberation,
  and what MacArthur's vow meant for the conduct of the Pacific war.

  Battle of Okinawa (1945): The last major island battle before the planned invasion
  of Japan. Kamikaze attacks at their peak, the highest US casualties of any Pacific
  battle, the mass civilian deaths. How Okinawa shaped the decision to use the atomic bomb.
