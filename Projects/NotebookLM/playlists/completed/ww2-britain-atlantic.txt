  Two battles, one video each. Apply the same lens to both:

  The RAF's narrow margin of survival, the role of radar, Churchill's rhetoric,
  and why Hitler called off the invasion. What made it so close.

  Battle of the Atlantic (1939-45): The longest continuous campaign of WW2.
  The U-boat wolf packs, the breaking of Enigma, the critical role of supply
  lines to Britain. Why Churchill called it the battle that frightened him most.
