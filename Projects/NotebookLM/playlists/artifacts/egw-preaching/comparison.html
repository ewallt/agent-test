Ellen G. White on Preaching

Two diagnostic comparisons drawn from her writings · White's actual verdicts in italics
1 · Christ-Centered vs. Legalistic Preaching
Attribute Christ-Centered Preaching Legalistic / Christless Preaching
Central Focus Jesus Christ — His grace, mercy, and righteousness — as the living center of every sermon. Doctrine is presented as revelation of Christ, not argument for conformity. Rules, arguments, proofs, and theological propositions without the person of Christ. Doctrine presented as obligation rather than as invitation to a Person.
Treatment of the Law Law and gospel presented hand-in-hand. The law acts as a mirror pointing to sin; Christ provides the remedy. Obedience flows from gratitude, not fear. Law emphasized as a list of requirements for acceptance. Gospel either absent or treated as a footnote to legal obligation. Behavior stressed without assurance.
Effect on the Soul Softens and subdues. Inspires hope and love. Leads hearers to lay hold of Christ's righteousness and experience genuine conversion. Produces dry formalism or discouragement. Hearers leave burdened or indifferent. "Nearly quenches the light and love of Jesus."
Assurance Offered Full assurance through Christ's imputed righteousness. The believer is accepted in the Beloved — not because of performance, but because of Christ. Assurance withheld or conditional. The hearer is left uncertain whether they have done enough, felt enough, or changed enough.
Historical Context The 1888 Minneapolis General Conference — E. J. Waggoner and A. T. Jones presenting Righteousness by Faith, strongly endorsed by White as a corrective to the prevailing trend. The drift White identified in Adventism toward argumentative, proof-text preaching that won debates but left congregations spiritually dry and Christless.
White's Verdict "Put Christ into every sermon. Let the preciousness, mercy, and glory of Jesus Christ be dwelt upon until Christ is formed within, the hope of glory." "There have been entire discourses, dry and Christless, in which Jesus has scarcely been named... The conviction of logic seldom softens and subdues the soul."
2 · Genuine Revival vs. Fanatical / Emotionalist Preaching
Attribute Genuine Revival Preaching Fanatical / Emotionalist Preaching
Source of Experience The Holy Spirit working quietly through the Word. Conviction comes through Scripture's claim on the conscience, not through manufactured atmosphere. Human excitement and manufactured atmosphere. Feelings generated by noise, repetition, and crowd psychology. Satan introduces counterfeits wherever God works.
Physical Expression Calmness, dignity, and godly fear. Deep solemnity. Quiet weeping and confession. A "solemn, abiding quietude in God." Jumping, shouting, dancing, screaming, speaking in unintelligible sounds. White called Ralph Mackin's 1908 "gift of tongues" episode "unmeaning gibberish" manufactured by human excitement.
Emotional Pattern Deep searching of heart, genuine remorse of soul, lasting peace. The joy that follows is grounded in assurance of forgiveness, not in the meeting's energy level. A "happy flight of feeling" followed by a crash. "When the happy flight of feeling is gone, they sink lower than before the meeting." The cycle repeats without transformation.
Relationship to Scripture Anchored in the Word. The preacher directs minds to the "solid Word of God" as the foundation of faith. Impressions are tested against Scripture. Relies on impressions, visions, and feelings as superior to Scripture. White: "Impressions and feelings are no sure evidence that a person is led by the Lord."
Lasting Effect Characters are transformed. Unity and love spread through the congregation. People go out as missionaries. The fruit remains after the meeting ends. Participants return to normal behavior, or worse. May boast of miracles while trampling the law. Creates division and discredits the movement in the eyes of outsiders.
White's Verdict "The most profitable meetings are not those with a happy flight of feeling, but those characterized by solemnity and deep searching of heart, confession of sin, and real humility." "Wherever God works, Satan introduces counterfeits to discredit the movement. These strange exercisings are unreliable guides planted by the great deceiver."
