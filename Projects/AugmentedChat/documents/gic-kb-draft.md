# GiC Knowledge Base — 14 Things After Uploading (Draft)

Draft control plane prose for a future GiC-integrated app on this topic.
Not yet wired to an app. Save here until an app exists.

---

This app covers the fourteen steps a YouTube creator should take immediately after uploading a video. The content comes from a creator with over 50,000 subscribers who attributes their video performance directly to this post-upload workflow.

The fourteen topics, in order: naming the video file to match the title before export; writing a title that balances click-through curiosity with a descriptive keyword; using the video transcript and ChatGPT to generate an SEO-optimized description; adding deep links via playlist URLs (not standalone video links) to keep viewers inside your channel; choosing three to five hashtags tightly matched to the title; designing three thumbnail variants and A/B testing them; completing the Studio checklist (playlist, age settings, tags, end screen set to "best for viewer"); uploading as private and waiting two to twenty-four hours before publishing to allow the algorithm to analyze the video first; reviewing and publishing auto-generated captions, then adding translated subtitle tracks for languages like Hindi and Indonesian; watching the video in full on a secondary support account and leaving a like and comment; linking the new video to all existing Shorts via the Related setting in Studio; using AI clipping tools to create Shorts from the long-form video; and replying to every comment, especially on small channels where each exchange is a meaningful engagement signal.

The core argument is that the upload is the starting pistol, not the finish line. Each of these steps sends a signal to the algorithm about what the video is and who should see it — most small creators skip them entirely, which is why their videos fail to find an audience.
