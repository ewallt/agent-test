# Illustration 02 — God Is Not a Criminal

**Source chapter:** Ch. 22
**Key argument:** If God used destructive force to compel obedience, He would operate on the same corrupt principles as a criminal organization. God relies only on goodness and love — never on compelling force.

---

## Story

A crime syndicate sends an enforcer to a local business owner demanding protection money. When the owner refuses, the syndicate escalates — smashing windows, burning the warehouse, threatening his family — until he is forced to submit.

The argument: if God's relationship to humanity worked the same way (obey or I destroy you), He would be indistinguishable from a criminal organization. God never operates by compulsion.

---

## Slides & Narration

### Slide 1 — The Setup: The Protection Racket
**Bullets:**
1. A crime syndicate sends an enforcer to a small business owner
2. The demand: pay protection money — or face the consequences
3. The owner refuses. He will not be extorted.

**Narration:**
> Imagine a crime syndicate operating in a small city. One day, they send an enforcer to a local business owner. The message is simple: pay protection money, or face consequences. The owner refuses. He will not be intimidated, and he will not be extorted. The enforcer leaves — but not empty-handed. He leaves with a warning.

### Slide 2 — The Escalation: The Enforcement Begins
**Bullets:**
1. First the windows. Then the warehouse. Then threats to his family.
2. Every act of violence is designed to break his will
3. The logic is simple: comply, or we will hurt you more

**Narration:**
> The consequences begin. First the windows are smashed. Then the warehouse burns. Then come threats against his family. Each act of escalating violence has one purpose: to break the owner's will through fear and pain. Comply, or we will hurt you more. This is the logic of coercion — of a power that rules through force.

### Slide 3 — The Question That Changes Everything: What Kind of God Is This?
**Bullets:**
1. Now apply that logic to God: Obey me — or I will destroy you
2. Plagues, floods, fire — wielded as enforcement tools
3. That god is not a protector. That god runs a protection racket.

**Narration:**
> Now apply that same logic to God. Obey me — or I will destroy you. Plagues on Egypt. Flood on the world. Fire on Sodom. Wielded as enforcement tools to compel submission. Wright puts the question plainly: if that is how God operates, what kind of God is this? That god is not a protector. That god is running a protection racket.

### Slide 4 — F.T. Wright's Argument: God Is Not a Criminal
**Bullets:**
1. God never uses compulsion — His only method is self-giving love
2. A god who destroys to compel obedience operates on Satan's principle
3. "Compelling power is found only under Satan's government. Not God's."

**Narration:**
> F.T. Wright's answer is unequivocal: God is not a criminal. God never uses compulsion — His only method is self-giving love. A god who destroys to compel obedience is not the God of Scripture. He operates on Satan's principle, not God's. As Ellen White wrote: Compelling power is found only under Satan's government. The Lord's principles are not of this order. God wins through revelation, through love — never through force.
