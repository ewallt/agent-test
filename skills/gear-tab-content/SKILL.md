# Gear-Tab Content Writer

Produces a structured analytical content document for a gear-tab app. The document has four parts that map naturally to tab-friendly sections. Content-agnostic — works for any topic.

## Step 1 — Understand the topic

Read what Tom gave you: a topic name, a description, pasted notes, or a source document.

Identify:
- **The subject** — what this content is about
- **The surface reading** — what a casual observer would assume about the topic
- **The real argument** — the hidden logic, unexpected structure, or deeper truth the document will reveal
- **Natural movements** — the key stages, dimensions, or sections of the topic (typically 4–8)

If the topic is rich enough to infer all of this clearly, proceed without asking.

If the real argument or movement structure is genuinely unclear, propose a specific interpretation and confirm with Tom in one message — don't ask multiple questions.

## Step 2 — Plan the movements

Before writing, sketch the movement titles and their guiding questions. Each movement answers a distinct question. The questions should form a logical sequence — together they trace the argument from surface to depth.

Confirm the plan with Tom only if there is real ambiguity. Otherwise proceed.
