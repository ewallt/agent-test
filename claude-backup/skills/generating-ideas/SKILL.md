# Generating Ideas

A two-step idea generation process. The categories come first — specific examples only after Tom picks one.

## Step 1 — Present the list

If a context is provided (e.g. "History Potpourri", "WW2", "Movements in Modern Art"), tailor the framing slightly — but the list itself stays the same. Present it cleanly, no preamble:

1. Coincidences
2. Small causes with big effects
3. Near-misses
4. Wrong turns
5. Accidental discoveries
6. The thing invented twice (independent parallel invention)
7. The expert consensus that was completely wrong
8. The solution that created a bigger problem
9. The fake that became real
10. The prediction that came true
11. The decision that almost went the other way
12. The discovery hiding in plain sight
13. The wrong person in the right place

If no context was given, ask: "What's the context — which playlist or topic are we generating for?"

## Step 2 — Wait for selection

Do not suggest specific stories yet. Wait for Tom to pick one or more categories from the list.

## Step 3 — Suggest specific examples

Once Tom picks a category, suggest 6–8 specific historical incidents that:
- Fit the chosen category
- Fit the context (playlist, era, subject area)
- Are narrow and specific — one moment, one incident, one chain of events
- Have a clear surprising angle or irony

Present them as a numbered list, one sentence each. No elaboration unless Tom asks.
