Scene-format sources translate into NLM cinematic videos with very high fidelity. The Adams/Jefferson July 4 video was evaluated as an "exceptionally faithful adaptation."

**Why:** NLM preserves structural sequence, specific details, direct quotes, and emotional arc almost exactly as written in the source. Write the scenes the way you want the video to feel — NLM will follow it closely.

**How to apply:**

- Include exact quotes — they will appear in the video (e.g., "Is it the Fourth yet?", "Thomas Jefferson survives" both made it verbatim)
- Include specific data points (ages, locations, counts) — they are retained
- Scene order is preserved; NLM may pull a detail forward slightly for pacing, but won't reorder scenes
- NLM adds visual graphics cues not in the source (timelines, dials) — no action needed, this is automatic
- Minor omissions happen at the margins for time — granular details that don't carry the main idea may be trimmed; don't rely on fine background detail for narrative weight
- The source scene sequence becomes the video's narrative arc — invest effort in getting scene order and split points right, since that's what shapes the final video
