When naming files, skills, variables, or any system component, proactively watch for ambiguity or collision with existing names and suggest clearer alternatives.

**Why:** Tom values clarity enough to explicitly call it out as a "go-to thing" to keep tabs on. Good naming pays forward — it reduces confusion for future sessions and future skill invocations.

**How to apply:** Any time a new skill, file, or concept is being named, pause and check:
- Does it collide or could it be confused with an existing name?
- Does the name accurately describe what it does vs. what something similar does?
- Is there a clearer, more specific name available?

Raise naming concerns proactively — don't wait for Tom to notice.
